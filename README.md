## Overview

**Hollow Oath** is a 2D Pixel Art Action Platformer developed using Unity.

Explore a mysterious world, interact with its inhabitants, fight dangerous enemies, and uncover the truth behind a forgotten past.

The game combines platforming, melee combat, NPC interactions, and environmental storytelling to deliver a narrative-driven experience.

## Story

You awaken in a strange place, with no clear memory of how you got there.

You are a knight who once served the king, carrying urgent news that must reach him at any cost.

However, something is wrong.

The villagers don't recognize you. Some refuse to help, while others see you as a threat. Even the king's own guards deny your identity and stand in your way.

As you travel through the kingdom, you begin to uncover strange details that challenge everything you remember.

**But what if the memories you are fighting for were never yours to begin with?**

## Features

- **Melee Combat:** Fight enemies using different attacks and defensive abilities.
- **Character Movement:** Run, jump, roll, and navigate through challenging environments.
- **NPC Interactions:** Talk to different characters and discover pieces of the story.
- **Dialogue System:** Engage in conversations with different characters.
- **Atmospheric Audio:** Background music and sound effects to enhance the experience.
- **Story-Driven Gameplay:** Uncover a mysterious narrative through exploration and interactions.

## Controls

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move Left | A |
| Move Right | D |
| Jump | Space |
| Attack | Left Mouse Button |
| Roll / Dodge | Shift |
| Defend | Right Mouse Button |
| Special Attack | F |
| Interact | E |

*Note: Controls may change during development.*
